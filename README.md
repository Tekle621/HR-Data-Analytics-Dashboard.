# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

**An interactive HR Analytics solution for workforce analysis, employee attrition, performance evaluation, and employee satisfaction.**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the development of a four-page interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes a fictional workforce of **1,325 employees** in an Ethiopian business context, providing insights to support workforce planning, employee retention, and HR decision-making.

**Dataset:** Synthetic data created using Microsoft Excel. No real employee information is included.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor workforce size and employee distribution.
- Analyze employee attrition and identify high-risk departments and job roles.
- Evaluate employee performance and manager ratings.
- Understand job satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Examine employee training participation.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Workforce Overview
- Total, active, and inactive employees.
- Employee attrition rate and average age.
- Hiring trends by year.
- Employee distribution by department and job role.

### 2. Performance Analysis
- Individual employee performance tracking.
- Self-assessment versus manager ratings.
- Employee performance and satisfaction trends.

### 3. Attrition Analysis
- Attrition by department and job role.
- Attrition by education and marital status.
- Business travel and years of service analysis.

### 4. Employee Ratings
- Job satisfaction analysis.
- Work-life balance ratings.
- Manager performance evaluations.
- Employee training participation.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **12.83% overall attrition:** 170 employees out of 1,325.
- **Highest departmental attrition:** Human Resources (19.23%).
- **Lowest departmental attrition:** Technology (9.74%).
- **Highest job-role attrition:** Recruiter (29.17%).
- Approximately **56% of reviews** indicated satisfied or very satisfied employees.
- Approximately **38% of reviews** indicated no training participation.

These findings illustrate how HR analytics can help identify workforce patterns and areas requiring further investigation.
